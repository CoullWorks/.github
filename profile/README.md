@danrcoull spent 18 years building software, 15 of them in ecommerce, most of it for global brands and most of it under NDA. COULLWORKS is where I put the tools I build for that work out in the open.

## What I build

I build for engineers like me.

**[keel](https://github.com/coullworks/keel)** is a project-launch studio for the terminal. Point it at a stack (Laravel, Magento, Symfony, Django, FastAPI, Next.js, Node) and it runs the *correct* command flow to set the project up properly, with the optimised configs in from the start. No getting to the end of a build and going "crap, I forgot the env config, or the Next.js image optimisation." AI will happily do all of it by hand and get half of it wrong. keel follows the flow that actually works.

**[Sonar](https://github.com/coullworks/sonar)** rides on top of keel and scans a project for AI-readiness, so AIO, GEO and SEO are built in at launch, not bolted on as an afterthought. It tells you, with evidence, whether an AI shopping agent can actually read and transact with your store.

There are a couple of **[browser extensions](https://github.com/coullworks/coullworks-extensions)** too: a stack detector, and an in-browser version of the Sonar audit that lives in your DevTools.

Go, recipes-as-data, agentic commerce, honest audits. All MIT, all in the open.

## Why it's open

I'm not here to gatekeep simplicity.

My ethos is impact: building things that help, including the small stuff. keel exists to take the Monday-morning-coffee stress out of starting a new project. The tools are free and they stay free. If they save you time and you want to chip in to keep them maintained, brilliant. I'm not going to charge you for something you could do yourself with an afternoon and enough coffee.
